# BOM notes

All costs are indicative estimates in USD at quantity 1 (September 2026) and will be checked against real quotes at TRL 3.

- Rows 1 to 10 are numbered to match the callouts in `media/exploded.png`. Rows 11 and 12 are not modeled.
- WellSense parts per well (rows 1 to 7, 9 and 10, with 30 m of cable and access tube): about $170.
- Row 8, the FieldNode core (about $126), is the lab's shared node and is costed in the FieldNode repo. With it, a complete logger is about $296. Whether the $180 budget covers the node is proposed, awaiting Amish (see `docs/REVIEW.md`).
- Row 11, the manual water level meter, is shared by a community or partner and is not in the per-well cost.
- The LoRaWAN gateway (TwinKit or a public network) is not included.
- Cable and access tube scale with probe depth: about $1.80 per metre of depth together.
